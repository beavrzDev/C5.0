# Business Function #5: Talent Operations & Recruiting

## What This Function Does
- **Category:** Enabling
- **Primary Owner (20 People):** Technical Recruiter & Operations
- **Primary Target KPI:** Time-to-hire $<30$ days; Offer acceptance rate $>85\%$

---

## Goals
1. **Specialized Talent Pipelines:** Proactive sourcing for specialized roles (Analytics Engineers, Spark/Databricks Data Engineers, FinOps Specialists).
2. **Hands-on Technical Vetting:** SQL/Python/IaC coding challenges rather than generic whiteboard algorithms.
3. **Clear Career Ladders & Retention:** Defined progression from Senior to Staff/Lead and Principal Architect with clear utilization targets.
4. **On-Demand Contractor Network:** Maintaining a pool of vetted 1099 contractors for specialized spikes (e.g., legacy mainframe to lakehouse migrations).

---

## How This Works
- **Recruitment Funnel & Sourcing:** Specialized online communities and technical open-source forums.
- **Technical Interview:** 3-stage evaluation (Principal Architect architectural review + practical hands-on challenge + cultural alignment).
- **Compensation Bands:** Market-leading base + pod gross margin performance bonus pool.
