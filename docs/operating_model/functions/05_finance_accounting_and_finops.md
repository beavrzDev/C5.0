# Business Function #5: Finance, Accounting & FinOps

## What This Function Does
- **Category:** Enabling
- **Primary Owner (20 People):** Operations & Finance Lead
- **Primary Target KPI:** Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) $<45$ days; Firm-wide Project Gross Margin $\ge 55\%$

---

## Goals
1. **Milestone-based billing:** Revenue recognized against SOW Definitions of Done (DoD), not hours.
2. **Pod Cost Accounting:** Real-time visibility into fully loaded sprint delivery costs (~\$32k/pod/sprint).
3. **FinOps Gain-share Calculation:** Verifiable measurement of client cloud compute savings and invoicing 20%–30% gain-share payouts.
4. **Cloud Vendor Credits & Cash Flow:** Managing partner sandbox credits and working capital.

---

## How This Works
- **Billing & Collections:** 50% upfront deposit on fixed-fee engagements; milestone triggers on outcome packages.
- **Pod Margin Reporting:** Weekly tracking of sprint velocity vs burn rate per pod.
- **FinOps Verification:** Baseline cloud cost metering, post-optimization audit, and contract gain-share reconciliation.
