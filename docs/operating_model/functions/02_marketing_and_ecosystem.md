# Business Function #2: Marketing & Brand Positioning

## What This Function Does
- **Category:** Core Revenue
- **Primary Owner (20 People):** Marketing Specialist / Operations
- **Primary Target KPI:** Inbound conversion rate & 100% MDF (Market Development Funds) claimed and deployed

---

## Goals
1. **Publish technical expertise:** Architectural breakdowns, benchmark studies, and lakehouse reference architectures.
2. **Co-Marketing with Partners (MDF):** Co-branded webinars, technical case studies, and sponsor presence at partner events (Databricks Data+AI Summit and cloud partner summits).
3. **Cloud Marketplace Presence:** Packaging services into AWS, Azure, and GCP Marketplaces to access enterprise committed cloud spend.

---

## How This Works
- **Content:** In-depth technical guides published bi-weekly.
- **MDF Lifecycle:** Proposal drafting, partner brand approval, execution, and lead reporting.
- **Client Case Studies:** Turn completed sprint milestones into measurable ROI stories (e.g., "\$300k compute reduction in 60 days via cluster optimization").
