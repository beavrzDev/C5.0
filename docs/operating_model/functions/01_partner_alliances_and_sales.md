# Business Function #1: Partner Alliances & Sales

## What This Function Does
- **Category:** Core Revenue
- **Primary Owner (20 People):** Alliance & Channel Sales Lead / Principal Data Architects
- **Primary Target KPI:** 50% – 60% partner-sourced pipeline

---

## Goals
1. **Sell through platform partnerships:** Build formal joint selling programs with Databricks and cloud partners (AWS, GCP, and Microsoft Azure).
2. **Account Executive & Partner Rep Alignment:** Direct relationships with regional partner account managers (PAMs) and enterprise account executives (AEs).
3. **Fixed-fee discovery as a sales entry point:** Use fixed-fee architecture audits (\$25,000 – \$45,000) as low-commitment entry points that lead to 3–5 sprint delivery pod engagements.

---

## How This Works
- **Partner Tiering:** Select / Registered → Premier → Elite milestones and Databricks certification requirements.
- **Deal Registration & Joint Selling Schedule:** CRM pipeline stages, partner portal registration, weekly pipeline syncing.
- **Incentive Alignment:** Rewarding partner reps through customer consumption acceleration and MDF utilization.
- **Handoff to Delivery:** Principal Architect discovery → Technical SOW scoping → Staffing check.
