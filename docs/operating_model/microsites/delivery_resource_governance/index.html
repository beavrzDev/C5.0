Section 01 Commercial Paradigm

Governed Fixed Capacity: Eliminating the T&M and Fixed-Price Traps

Midmarket clients reserve dedicated, autonomous Databricks delivery pods in 12-week quarterly capacity blocks. Billing is pre-paid per sprint unit, eliminating timesheet disputes while shielding the firm from client-side onboarding delays.

TRADITIONAL T&M The Efficiency Penalty

Hourly Billing (T&M)

Incentivizes billing hours over velocity, penalizing efficiency and client trust.

  • ✕
    Adversarial Margins: Building automated lakehouse pipelines or deploying reusable IP finishes projects faster and destroys billable consultant hours.
  • ✕
    Commodity Friction: Client procurement reduces senior engineers to interchangeable rate cards, inviting constant pressure from offshore vendors.
  • ✕
    Administrative Drag: Constant disputes over 15-minute timecard increments and individual consultant productivity.
FIXED-PRICE TRAP The Unhedged Scope Trap

Milestone Sign-Off

Shifts all client organizational friction, credential blockers, and scope ambiguity onto firm payroll.

  • ✕
    Client Dependency Stalls: Slow client IAM approvals, schema changes, or missing test data halt sprint DoD sign-offs and lock up invoice releases.
  • ✕
    Firm Absorbs 100% Downside: Client organizational friction and delays are absorbed entirely by consulting payroll with no billing relief.
  • ✕
    Scope Creep Escalation: Subjective interpretation of "Done" causes friction at milestone boundaries and delays project completion.
C5.0 OPERATING MODEL Governed Capacity

Governed Fixed Capacity

Aligns client business success directly with pod velocity, pre-paid security, and guaranteed margin realization.

  • ✓
    Pre-Paid Sprint Reservation: Sprints are invoiced and collected prior to Day 1 of each 2-week cycle, securing zero DSO and predictable cash flow.
  • ✓
    Autonomous Squad Governance: Clients prioritize the product backlog; our Pod Leads own architecture reviews, PR gating, and execution velocity.
  • ✓
    12-Week Quarterly Blocks: 6-sprint minimum commitments eliminate project bench churn and give midmarket clients budgetable OpEx line items.
💡
Commercial Mandate:

Clients purchase dedicated engineering capacity committed to an agreed Target Milestone Backlog under the Databricks Medallion Architecture. Billing is fixed based on reserved capacity—not individual timesheets or client approval delays.

Section 03 Scale Architecture (35–60 FTEs)

Transitioning to Dedicated Clusters & Resource Operations Hub

Preventing communication bottlenecks and governance dilution as the practice scales from 3 to 10+ active delivery pods. Core (3-FTE) and Scale (5-FTE) pods co-exist seamlessly within governed 3-pod clusters.

SCALING PILLAR 01 Distributed Delivery

Dedicated 3-Pod Technical Clusters

Beyond 35 FTEs, a single governance pair cannot effectively oversee the growing roster. C50 groups pods into autonomous 3-pod clusters (whether Core or Scale) led by dedicated senior officers:

  • •
    Cluster Lead Architect (1.0 FTE Dedicated): Holds exclusive oversight over 3 client pods. Leads presales technical discovery and conducts weekly architecture gate reviews.
  • •
    Cluster Delivery Lead (1.0 FTE Dedicated): Tracks velocity burn, unblocks cross-squad dependencies, and enforces pre-paid invoicing schedules.
  • •
    Cluster Risk Pooling: If a client backlog is paused due to upstream delays, pod capacity is absorbed by sister pods in the cluster before triggering bench status.
SCALING PILLAR 02 Central Staffing

Central Resource Operations Hub

Separating individual project delivery management from company-wide capacity planning, skill-based assignment, and managing unassigned time:

  • •
    Resource Operations Lead (Hire at 35 FTEs): Dedicated owner of matching people to projects, keeping people assigned, and 6-week forward capacity booking.
  • •
    Automated CRM & PSA Capacity Locking: Deals reaching 70%+ win probability in the CRM platform automatically trigger provisional pod allocations in the resource scheduling system.
  • •
    Granular Skill Inventory Matching: Staffing matches cloud competency tags (Databricks, Spark, Streaming, SQL, Python) rather than interchangeable headcount.

Section 05 Work Distribution

The 80/10/10 Capacity Allocation Engine

Balancing dedicated client sprint backlog velocity with senior-led mentoring and reusable Brickbuilder asset creation.

🎯
Client Presentation & Capacity Rule:

Externally, clients purchase 100% of the pod's sprint commitment. Internally, pod velocity estimation points are budgeted to reserve 80% for direct client milestones, 10% for technical upskilling, and 10% for reusable Brickbuilder IP.

10% Mentoring (8h)
10% IP (8h)

80%

Direct Client Backlog Execution

64 hours per cycle per FTE dedicated exclusively to contractual Databricks Medallion milestones. Engineers are shielded from non-pod meetings, administrative overhead, and context switching.

10%

Technical Shadowing & Upskilling

8 hours per cycle per FTE. Mid-level engineers shadow senior architects on complex Spark streaming pipelines, Unity Catalog governance configurations, and enterprise security setups without billing the client.

10%

Practice Lab & Reusable IP

8 hours per cycle per FTE reserved to harvest generic Databricks Asset Bundles (DABs), dbt macros, CI/CD templates, and Terraform lakehouse modules that accelerate future onboarding cycles.
