Section 01 Operating Approach

From Hourly Billing to Pod Velocity & Outcome-Based Delivery

Clients pay for completed outcomes — not engineering hours. Capacity is tracked as committed vs. uncommitted pod sprints across a 6-week rolling horizon.

OLD APPROACH T&M Model

Legacy Hourly Billing (T&M)

Rewards time spent instead of results, and leaves the firm exposed to falling hourly rates.

  • ✕
    Unit of Measure: The billable hour, with individual billability targets of about 80%.
  • ✕
    Utilization Pressure: Revenue depends on keeping every engineer billable. Any gap between projects is unrecovered cost, which pushes the firm to staff by availability instead of fit.
  • ✕
    The Efficiency Penalty: If the engineering squad builds automated reusable code and finishes faster, billable revenue plummets.
  • ✕
    Client Friction: Clients focus on the hourly rate, not the result. Offshore firms and AI-driven expectations of lower rates keep pushing that rate down.
C5.0 OPERATING MODEL DoD Velocity

Outcome-Based Pod Velocity

Aligns client business success directly with consultant velocity and margin expansion.

  • ✓
    Unit of Measure: Pod Sprint Velocity (bi-weekly sprint units) & contractual Definition of Done (DoD) milestones.
  • ✓
    100% Dedicated Delivery Pods: Zero fractional assignments within the core squad; pods execute against a single clear client backlog.
  • ✓
    Growing Margins (≥55%): Using reusable IP and automated templates accelerates delivery, expanding gross margin without client friction.
  • ✓
    Client Value Alignment: Clients buy guaranteed operational lakehouses, streaming pipelines, and transformation models with zero timesheet arguments.
💡

Core Operating Mandate: Clients buy predictable cloud data outcomes and sprint velocity—not engineering hours. Internal capacity is governed by tracking Committed vs. Uncommitted Pod Sprints across a 6-week rolling horizon.

Section 03 Scale Architecture (35–60 FTEs)

Transitioning to Dedicated Clusters & Resource Operations Hub

Preventing communication bottlenecks and governance dilution as the practice scales from 3 to 10+ active delivery pods.

SCALING PILLAR 01 Distributed Delivery

Dedicated 3-Pod Technical Clusters

Beyond 35 FTEs, a shared governance overlay become too much for one person to manage. C50 groups pods into autonomous 3-pod clusters led by dedicated senior officers:

  • •
    Cluster Lead Architect (1.0 FTE Dedicated): Assigned exclusively across 3 pods for high-risk system design, architectural governance, and escalation management.
  • •
    Cluster Delivery Lead (1.0 FTE Dedicated): Governs sprint velocity, milestone gate releases, and client account health across the 3 pods.
  • •
    Cluster Risk Pooling: If a pod finishes early, capacity is absorbed by other pods in the same cluster before going unassigned.
SCALING PILLAR 02 Central Staffing

Central Resource Operations Hub

Separating individual project delivery management from company-wide capacity planning, skill-based assignment, and managing unassigned time:

  • •
    Resource Operations Lead (Hire at 35 FTEs): Dedicated owner of matching people to projects, keeping people assigned, and 6-week forward capacity booking.
  • •
    Automated CRM & PSA Pipeline Hold: Deals reaching 70%+ win probability in the CRM platform automatically trigger provisional pod allocations in the resource scheduling system.
  • •
    Granular Skill Inventory Matching: Staffing matches cloud competency tags (Databricks, Spark, Streaming, SQL, Python) rather than interchangeable headcount.

Section 06 Quality & Growing Reusable Assets

Binary Definition of Done (DoD) & Turning Idle Time into Reusable Assets

Automating release verification while directing unassigned time into building reusable assets.

GATE 01 Day 1–3

Code & Template Gate

Automated deployment of standard Infrastructure-as-Code and lakehouse project templates. Mandatory peer PR reviews required on 100% of merges.

GATE 02 Day 4–8

Automated QA Gate

100% pass rate across automated schema tests, data freshness checks, and integration tests running in CI/CD runner environments.

GATE 03 Day 9

FinOps & Security Audit

Verification of lakehouse query performance, compute cost limits, PII masking compliance, and zero client data retained locally.

GATE 04 Release

DoD Milestone Sign-Off

Objective contractual milestone verification signed by client sponsor, unlocking milestone invoice release independently of hours logged.
