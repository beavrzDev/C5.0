Section 01 Commercial Model

Prescriptive Outcomes Powered by Governed Pod Capacity

Midmarket clients buy defined, bounded cloud data milestones. Internal delivery operates on dedicated, autonomous sprint pods—eliminating hourly timesheet friction while protecting delivery margins.

HOURLY T&M The Efficiency Penalty

Hourly Billing (T&M)

Billing hours penalizes speed; using automated code or templates destroys billable revenue.

  • ✕
    Adversarial Margins: Automated pipelines and reusable IP finish projects faster, which directly reduces billable revenue in hourly contracts.
  • ✕
    Commodity Friction: Client procurement reduces senior engineers to interchangeable rate cards, inviting constant pressure from offshore vendors.
  • ✕
    Administrative Overhead: Constant disputes over 15-minute timecard increments and individual consultant productivity rather than outcome velocity.
UNHEDGED FIXED PRICE The Delivery Trap

Unhedged Fixed Price

Open-ended scope and client credential/environment delays are absorbed 100% by firm payroll.

  • ✕
    Client Dependency Stalls: Slow client IAM approvals, schema changes, or missing test environments halt sprint DoD sign-offs and lock up invoice releases.
  • ✕
    Firm Absorbs 100% Downside: Client organizational friction and external stalls are absorbed entirely by consulting payroll with no billing relief.
  • ✕
    Scope Creep Escalation: Subjective interpretations of "Done" cause friction at milestone boundaries and compress project margins.
C5.0 GOVERNED PODS The Sweet Spot

Prescriptive Outcomes & Governed Pods

Prescriptive deliverables defined as an agreed Target Milestone Backlog estimated in velocity points and executed across fixed-capacity 2-week sprints—not an unhedged fixed-price warranty. Sprint capacity is reserved; client owns backlog priority, while Pod Leader owns technical quality gates.

  • ✓
    Pre-Paid Sprint Reservation: Sprints are invoiced and collected prior to Day 1 of each 2-week cycle, securing zero DSO and predictable cash flow.
  • ✓
    Dedicated Sprint Capacity: Client owns product backlog priorities; our Pod Leaders own architecture reviews, automated PR gating, and delivery velocity.
  • ✓
    12-Week Quarterly Blocks: 6-sprint minimum commitments eliminate project bench churn and give midmarket clients budgetable OpEx line items.

Section 03 Scale Architecture (20–60 FTEs)

Scaling via Autonomous 3-to-4 Pod Delivery Clusters

Phase 1 reaches maximum operational efficiency at 4 Core Pods (14 people total) under a single cluster leadership pair. Phase 2 begins when Pod 5 launches (~20+ headcount), triggering Cluster 2 and formally funding the dedicated Central Resource Operations Leader. Delivery capacity scales horizontally up to 60 FTEs without bureaucratic overhead.

SCALING PILLAR 01 Cluster Topologies

Modular Delivery Clusters & Leadership Swarming

Delivery clusters function as modular capacity envelopes governing 12 to 15 delivery engineers. Pods remain standardized, while cluster leadership flexes across three supported topologies:

1. Core Midmarket Cluster (14 People Total) 80% Active Load

4 Core Pods (12 Engineers) governed by 1 Principal Architect + 1 Delivery Manager operating at an optimal 80% active oversight load (4 × 0.20 FTE).

2. Scale Enterprise Cluster (17 People Total) 75%–100% Load

3 Scale Pods (15 Engineers) governed by 1 Principal Architect (75% load) + 1 Delivery Manager (100% load) for high-throughput streaming and complex governance.

3. Composite Hybrid Cluster (13–16 People Total) 65%–75% Load

Mixed client portfolios (e.g., 2 Midmarket Core Pods + 1 Enterprise Scale Pod = 11 delivery engineers). Maintains leadership utilization between 65% and 75% without reorganizing squads or hiring extra overhead.

⚡ Cluster Leadership Swarming & Dedicated Squad Isolation:
  • •
    Dedicated Squad Isolation: Delivery squads remain 100% dedicated to their client backlog and never cross account boundaries, strictly preserving client data isolation and NDAs.
  • •
    Stop-the-Clock Protocol: If a client encounters IAM or environment stalls, the pod pivots to secondary backlog items or internal Brickbuilder IP (billing continues uninterrupted).
  • •
    Leadership Shock Absorber: The cluster leadership pair shifts excess oversight hours to swarm sister pods, accelerating complex code reviews, DAB promotions, and production deployment gates.
⏱️ Staggered Pod Intake Rule
≥ 1 Sprint Separation
Within any delivery cluster, new pod kickoffs must be staggered by a minimum of one 2-week sprint cycle. A single Principal Architect cannot onboard more than one new client pod concurrently—preventing front-loaded intake spikes (40%–60% Arch load) from creating architectural governance or PR review bottlenecks.
