## Growth Phases

### Phase 1: 20 → 35 People (Platform Foundation)
* **Goal:** Build undeniable technical credibility and strong partner joint selling momentum with Databricks and cloud partners.
* **What Changes:**
  - Standardize reusable codebase templates (Terraform modules, lakehouse project starters) to cut deployment setup by 40%.
  - Split the combined Delivery/Resourcing role by hiring a dedicated **Resource Operations Lead**.

### Phase 2: 35 → 60 People (Practice Groups & Nearshore Expansion)
* **Goal:** Protect margins as salaries rise while expanding into real-time streaming and AI/MLOps on Databricks.
* **What Changes:**
  - Launch blended nearshore delivery pods (e.g., LatAm or Eastern Europe) led by onshore Lead Architects.
  - Split into 4 specialized teams: Lakehouse Platform, Real-Time Streaming, FinOps & Governance, and MLOps / AI Engineering.

### Phase 3: 60 → 100 People (Enterprise Scale & Recurring Revenue)
* **Goal:** Become a stable, globally capable business with strong recurring revenue.
* **What Changes:**
  - Grow SLA-backed Managed DataOps retainers to 35%+ of total firm revenue.
  - List proprietary data accelerators directly on AWS, Azure, and GCP Marketplaces to transact enterprise cloud commitment drawdowns.
