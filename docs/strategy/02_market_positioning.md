## Why Specialization Wins

### 1. Selling through Platform Partners
Rather than building an expensive outbound sales team, C50 sells through platform partner organizations. When data platforms (Databricks, AWS, Azure, GCP) sell consumption commitments, they need qualified implementation partners to activate those workloads. C50 is their deployment partner.

### 2. Dedicated Specialized Roles
Traditional software consultancies treat data as an afterthought. C50 treats these as dedicated disciplines:
* **Analytics Engineering:** Bridging raw lakehouse engineering and business intelligence through automated transformation modeling and semantic layers.
* **DataOps & FinOps:** Automating CI/CD for pipelines while keeping cloud compute and cluster costs under control.

### 3. Why This Is Hard to Copy
Building production data systems that handle strict data consistency, regulatory compliance (PII/HIPAA), real-time streaming, and petabyte-scale lakehouses requires deep domain expertise. This keeps rates high and clients loyal.
